## 1. One-paragraph summary

**Phoenix Fire** is an online esports football (soccer) club that plays **EA SPORTS FC Pro Clubs** on new-generation platforms (PS5, Xbox Series, PC; the EA data calls the platform `common-gen5`). It is a competitive but friendly community: players control one footballer each in team modes (Rush 5v5 and 11v11 league play), organised through a Discord server. Motto: **"Rise. Burn. Repeat."** The club was founded in **2025**. Its main EA club ID is **1111**. It plays its home games at a custom stadium called **The Firepit**.

## 3. The game and the modes (for readers who do not know Pro Clubs)

- **EA SPORTS FC Pro Clubs**: instead of one person controlling the whole team, each human controls **one player** (a custom-built "Pro") on the pitch. A full team is up to 11 humans. Positions matter: GK (goalkeeper), CB (centre back), CDM / CM / CAM (defensive, central, attacking midfield), W (winger), ST (striker).
- **League / divisions (11v11)**: EA's online seasons with divisions. Teams earn points, get promoted or relegated. Phoenix tracks its record from EA's own data.
- **Rush (5v5)**: small-sided mode with 5 humans a side. Matchmaking is thin, so when two Phoenix groups search at once they can end up matching each other. To avoid that the club runs a one-at-a-time queue (groups of up to 5 take turns searching).
- **Friendlies, playoffs and tournaments**: organised on match nights; players confirm attendance and a manager sets the line-up.
- A "lobby" is the group of players forming a team for a session. Players mark themselves **available** when they want a game; others invite them.

## 6. Culture and philosophy (the club's own words, summarised)

- **The club comes first.** No player, manager or personal stat record is above the club.
- **We want to win, but we do not need perfect players. We need good teammates.** Consistency in how people represent the club matters more than flawless play or unlimited free time.
- **Competition without pressure to be perfect.** Good decisions, playing your role, helping teammates, learning from hard games.
- **Fair chances, shared responsibility.** A spot in a competitive line-up is earned by contribution, availability and what the squad needs, never favouritism or friendship. Managers explain selection openly.
- **Rise. Burn. Repeat.** A bad night is not the end of anything: learn, reset, come back together.
- Standards apply to everyone, management included. Mistakes, jokes, respectful disagreement and off days are fine.

## 7. House rules (short form)

1. Respect every member; banter only while it is mutual. 2. Play for the team (use teammates, do not force solo plays). 3. Keep your position and the agreed setup. 4. Never sabotage matches (no own goals, trolling, rage-quitting to punish). 5. Keep match comms constructive (call runs, no constant blame). 6. Be fair with lobby spots and rotations; nobody owns a lobby. 7. Follow session requirements (connection, platform, availability). 8. Honour match-night commitments; repeated no-shows can affect selection. 9. Follow rules of each competition. 10. Respect decisions but you may question them privately. 11. No favouritism or misuse of staff roles. 12. Protect the community (no sharing private info, no impersonation). 13. Accept feedback and own mistakes. 14. Issues are handled by context: conversation, warning, temporary exclusion from organised sessions, removal of a staff role, or removal from the club; the owner can review any decision.

## 8. Joining

- **Trials are by application only** (applications go through the Discord server).
- Requirements: active on PS5 / Xbox Series / PC (new gen); available for regular league nights; mic and Discord for match communication; a competitive attitude ("we play to win").
- Positions the club looks for: GK, CB, CDM, CM, CAM, ST.
- Managers assess trial players fairly. Nobody is promised a starting place or a favourite position.

## 9. How a normal week feels

- Members drop into the Discord, say when they are free for Rush or league, and form lobbies.
- Match nights are organised: players confirm attendance, a manager posts the line-up with bench, results are posted afterwards.
- Players follow their own stats (goals, assists, ratings, win rate) and the club's results; the club celebrates form and does not shame bad nights.
- Voice chat is expected during matches.

## 11. Tips for another model writing or reasoning about Phoenix Fire

- Say **Phoenix Fire** or **Phoenix**; never "NOREX". Use "club" and "squad" for the team, "Pro Clubs" for the mode.
- Keep claims grounded. If you do not know a fact (history, league names, schedule, staff), say so or ask the owner instead of guessing.
- Match the voice: confident, warm, team-first. Use the motto sparingly.
- Orange and near-black are the colours; the phoenix crest is the symbol.
- Do not publish members' private data (gamertags are fine inside the club; avoid real names, addresses, contact details).
